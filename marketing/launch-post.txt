Your filters. A little chaos. Your wallet's final say.

Built at NEAR HACKATHON.

>> Define your token universe

Choose the conditions before the reveal: token age, market cap, liquidity, holder count and recent buy/sell activity. Use presets to get started, sliders to explore and exact values when you know what you want.

Current screening rules exclude flagged and unknown risk results. Screening cannot guarantee safety or returns.

>> Pick your mischief

Turn the crank on a 3D lottery cage. Open a case. Go Full Degen through Chain, Token and Amount.

A free reveal buys nothing. See the selected token, inspect its chart and review the available route. You decide whether to trade, with an explicit approval in your wallet.

>> Pass your setup around

Degen Codes let friends share their filter rules. Copy a setup, load it, inspect the settings, tweak them and Apply.

A code carries no wallet authority or trade amount. It shares your rules; changing market data can still produce a different pool. Degen Codes are implemented in the public source, with hosted rollout pending.

>> Follow the trade beyond the reveal

Charts, receipts, portfolio views and a points leaderboard keep the journey in one place. Eligible verified Solana USDC buys earn proportional points after execution.

>> Give your agent a way to inspect the choices

Read-only MCP tools in the source expose market observations, explain why tokens are excluded from a pool and request dry quotes. A dry quote moves no funds. Wallet approval remains a separate step.

>> Help build the arcade

The Contributor DAO is live on NEAR testnet inside the original app.

Council votes authorize a review of an exact merged pull request and its beneficiary. An appointed deterministic worker submits a score and evidence digest. Challenge rules govern the contributor's reward claim, while the agent's service fee is accounted separately.

Our real-PR acceptance run finalized 40 contributor test tokens and a 1-token agent fee. The transactions are public. These test tokens have no monetary value.

>> What NEAR makes possible

NEAR enforces treasury budgets, review authorization and reward claims. NEAR Intents supports cross-chain quote and deposit-review flows. Our policy/Chain Signatures work also demonstrates bounded testnet execution.

The direction is a community-built arcade: share useful settings, help improve the product and make contribution rewards inspectable.

Community preset discovery, creator rewards and private trading are next steps. Confidential quote access, funded mainnet acceptance and hosted OutLayer/NEAR AI execution remain unfinished.
