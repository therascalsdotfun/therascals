TheRascalsDotFun is a memecoin discovery-to-trade arcade built for degens who have a strategy but are tired of chasing charts and other people's calls. The idea: define your universe first, then let a playful reveal make the next choice.

YOUR FILTERS - Presets, sliders and exact limits shape a screened pool using token age, market cap, liquidity, holders and recent buy/sell activity. Flagged and unknown risk results are excluded by the current screening rules. Screening is not a guarantee of safety or returns.

YOUR MISCHIEF - Turn a physical 3D lottery cage, open a case or go Full Degen across Chain, Token and Amount. Free reveals buy nothing. Review the selected asset, chart and route before approving a trade in your wallet.

YOUR COMMUNITY - Degen Codes in the public source let users copy, load, inspect and apply one another's filter settings without sharing wallet authority. Read-only MCP tools give agents access to market observations, pool explanations and dry quotes. These source features are distinct from the hosted release.

BUILD THE ARCADE TOGETHER - Our NEAR testnet Contributor DAO turns merged work into governed reward claims. Council votes authorize the exact PR, merge and beneficiary. A deterministic review worker submits a score and evidence digest; challenge rules gate contributor and agent payments. A real-PR acceptance run finalized 40 contributor test tokens and a 1-token agent fee.

NEAR enforces the DAO's treasury, budgets and claims. NEAR Intents provides supported cross-chain quote/deposit-review flows; earlier policy/Chain Signatures work demonstrates bounded testnet execution. Confidential quotes, funded mainnet acceptance and hosted OutLayer/NEAR AI remain unfinished.

Try the free arcade and testnet DAO at https://therascals.fun. Public MIT source includes the implementation, tests and receipts.
